For emergence — the first moment a portrait becomes visible without explanation — Constellation is stronger. Relationships appear as nearness; the whole is not yet named. Shared Baseline is the more restrained and dignified chorus, but it feels like one instrument already tuned — closer to voice than to a portrait beginning to gather.

Archive returns. Collection should not return again — it should let likeness start to cohere. That coherence starts in Constellation’s field, not on Baseline’s single line.
