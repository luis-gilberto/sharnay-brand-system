**Emergence:** Care + steadiness. Reads as one temperament. **Pass.**

### Collection 2

**Emergence:** Anti-hustle voice. Strong pair. **Pass.**

### Collection 3
- Q12: *Seen without being performed.*

**Emergence:** Soft resistance to performance. **Pass.**

### Collection 4
- Q12: *Like I was paying attention the whole time.*
- Q24: *Like my real voice, not a studio voice.*  
**Emergence:** Attention + authorship. Slightly explanatory but still human. **Pass.**

### Collection 5
- Q12: *Relieved.*
- Q24: *Hushed.*  
**Emergence:** Thin alone, but together they still form a quiet mood — weaker but not broken. **Conditional pass** (edge case of short answers).

**Collection verdict:** With natural-length answers, Q12 + Q24 produce Emergence. They do not require a third Voice question to feel complete at `minFragments: 2`. Optional third Voice remains unnecessary for freeze.

**Failure mode to watch in production:** both answers as three-adjective slogans (“Elegant. Effortless.” + “Clean. Modern. Bold.”) — rare if helper text is read; still better than boundary words like “Cheap.”
