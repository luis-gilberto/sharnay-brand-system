# Social

**The system rendered at speed, and in volume.**

Opens in **Phase 5**. Currently empty.

---

This is the surface where the brand is seen most often and controlled least. It is also where identity systems usually break, because the person posting is tired, on a phone, and in a hurry.

Everything designed here is judged by one standard: can it be used well under those conditions? A template that requires patience is a template that will be abandoned by the third week.

---

## What will live here

- Grid strategy — the feed composed as a contact sheet rather than a queue
- Post and carousel templates
- Story templates for behind-the-scenes, booking, and release
- Caption conventions: how work is titled, credited, located, and dated
- A working method for preparing a week of posts in one sitting
- Exported, ready-to-use template files in the formats Sharnay actually works in

## What will not

One-off posts. Individual campaign artwork. Anything that is not a reusable system piece.

## Standards, set now

**Few templates, strictly held.** A large template library becomes a soft library within a month.

**A single image gets room.** The most common post is one photograph with nothing around it, and the system should make that the easiest thing to do.

**No improvisation per post.** No filters, no ad-hoc borders, no type placed by feel.

**Everything survives cropping.** The platform will crop it. Design for that from the start.

## The test for this phase

Sharnay produces a week of posts in under an hour, without a designer, and the result is indistinguishable from something we made.
