# Documentation

The written system. Six documents, in reading order.

---

These are not notes about the work. They are the work — the decisions, and the reasoning that makes those decisions extendable by someone who was not in the room.

**01 · [Project Overview](project-overview.md)**
Vision, goals, philosophy, deliverables, and the questions still open with the client.

**02 · [Roadmap](roadmap.md)**
Seven phases, ordered by dependency. What each produces and what completion means.

**03 · [Visual Language System](visual-language-system.md)**
The eight systems that make up the identity, described by what they must accomplish. Intent, not execution.

**04 · [The Photographer's Vocabulary](photographer-vocabulary.md)**
Light, frame, focus, perspective, depth, print. The conceptual foundation everything else is built on.

**05 · [Design Principles](design-principles.md)**
Ten principles, each with a test. The north star, and the tiebreaker.

**06 · [Implementation Plan](implementation-plan.md)**
The creative order of operations, and why the sequence is what it is.

## Where to start

**New to the project** — read 04, then 03. The vocabulary explains why the systems are shaped the way they are, and the systems make far more sense in that order.

**Joining mid-engagement** — read 05 and 06. The principles settle most questions; the plan explains where the work currently stands.

**Presenting to the client** — 01 and 02, in that order.

---

## How these documents are kept

Revisions are lettered. Revision A is the initial draft; each substantive revision advances the letter and notes what changed.

Decisions are recorded where the system they affect is documented, alongside the alternative that was rejected. The rejected option is usually the more useful record.

Superseded documents move to [`/archive`](../archive) rather than being overwritten.
