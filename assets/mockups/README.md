# Mockups

Presentation renders and photographed pieces — how the work is shown before it exists, and documented after it does.

Opens in **Phase 2**. Currently empty.

---

**Holds** — mockup renders for presentations, photographs of physical proofs, and scene setups used across reviews.

**Does not hold** — the design files being mocked up. Those live with their system.

**Standard** — mockups are photographic, plain, and honest. No floating cards on gradients, no artificial reflections, no scenes styled to flatter a weak idea. If a mark needs a beautiful mockup to look good, the mark is the problem.

Photographed proofs are preferred to renders wherever a physical piece exists. A real card on real paper under real light is more persuasive than any render, and it is also a truer test.

**Naming** — `mockup-business-card-v01.jpg` · `proof-print-sleeve-2026-09-02.jpg`

Consistency matters here: one lighting setup, one surface, one point of view across a presentation. Mixed mockup styles make a coherent system look like a collection.
