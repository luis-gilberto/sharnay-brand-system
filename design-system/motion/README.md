# Motion

**Optical, not digital.** Movement drawn from lenses and light rather than from interface convention.

Opens in **Phase 2**, as principle. Implemented in **Phase 4**. Currently empty.

---

## What lives here

- The behaviour set: focus, exposure, shutter, aperture, depth
- The duration scale and its physical basis
- The easing family — one curve set for the whole system
- Prototypes, at the minimum fidelity needed to prove a behaviour works
- Reduced-motion equivalents, designed rather than disabled
- Timing documentation written so a developer can implement it exactly

## What does not

Animation built for a specific page — that belongs to `/website`. Decorative loops. Anything that is enjoyable to watch twice.

## Standards

Things resolve, expose, and open. They do not slide, bounce, pop, or spring.

Durations come from a fixed scale with a stated physical reference. Arbitrary values are how a system starts to feel uncalibrated even when nobody can say why.

Motion clarifies sequence and relationship, then stops. Every behaviour is tested on a phone at real image sizes, because that is where most of it will actually be seen and where most of it will fail.
