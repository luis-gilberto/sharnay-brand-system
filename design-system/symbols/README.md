# Symbols

**The generative core.** The aperture construction, and the marks that come out of it.

Opens in **Phase 2**. Currently empty.

---

## What lives here

- Aperture construction drawings — blade count, rotation logic, the derived angle set
- Primary mark, in its master form
- Monogram and any reduced form
- Lockups: mark with wordmark, horizontal and stacked
- Clear space, minimum size, and reproduction rules
- Incorrect usage, drawn honestly
- Exploration and rejected directions, kept until the phase closes

## What does not

Application of the mark to real pieces — that belongs to `/website`, `/social`, or `/print`. Distributable logo files — those are exported to `/assets/logos`. Anything sampled from a competitor.

## Standards

Every form is constructed, not sketched: circles, lines, and the aperture's angle set only. The construction drawing is a deliverable in its own right, because the mark must be rebuildable from first principles by someone who has never met us.

Approval requires the mark to survive 12mm in one colour, 512px on screen, reversed out of a photograph, and embossed without ink.
