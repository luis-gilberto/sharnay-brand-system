# Print

**The system rendered in the hand.**

Opens in **Phase 6**. Currently empty.

---

Print goes last because it is irreversible. Nothing should be committed to paper until the language has been proven on two other surfaces.

It also matters more than its size suggests. A card is held, turned over, and judged in under two seconds, and paper weight is a more persuasive argument than anything printed on it. This is where a premium positioning is either confirmed or quietly contradicted.

---

## What will live here

- Business card
- Print sleeve or folio for delivered work
- Thank-you card, and a seal or sticker
- Pricing and services guide
- Packaging direction
- Full production specifications: stock, weight, colour, finish, tolerance
- Press-ready artwork and a proofing checklist
- Printer correspondence and quotes

## What will not

Digital-only documents. Speculative pieces with no place in the client's actual working life.

## Standards, set now

**Producible at a sane cost.** A system requiring an unreasonable print budget is a system that gets abandoned after the first order.

**One finish at a time.** Foil, emboss, and duplex do not appear together on the same piece.

**Specified for a printer.** In their terms, with tolerances, not as a mood board with a note attached.

**Uncoated by default**, unless a specific piece argues otherwise.

## Approval

Physical proofs only. A print piece is approved in the hand or not at all — no piece in this folder is signed off from a screen.
